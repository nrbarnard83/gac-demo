GAC Counter Planner

Picks counters for an opponent's predicted defense, then builds your own defense from the units left over. It runs in your browser on your own swgoh.gg login, and nothing is uploaded. See a sample report

Free to use, built by a guild leader in his spare time.If it saves you time in GAC, a small tip is welcome and never expected. Support this project
You need: a swgoh.gg account that can open GAC Insight (Player+, or a guild with Guild Patron) and a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).

Set up (once)

  1. Sign in at swgoh.gg in your browser.
  2. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).
  3. Drag this button up onto the bar and let go: GAC Planner
    Drag it, do not click it. Hold the mouse button on it, move up to the bookmarks bar, and release.

Run it

  1. Open any swgoh.gg page. It does nothing on a New Tab or any other site.
  2. Click the "GAC Planner" bookmark. It finds you and your opponent by itself: your current round's opponent, or your last one between weeks.
  3. Wait about 20 seconds. The report opens over the page. Download page saves it, and Choose another opponent switches who it plans against.
Reading the report
Attack with
What the opponent is expected to place (with the datacron perks they usually put on it), and the squad of yours that beats it. A counter is only offered if it wins at least 75% of the time in Kyber and its safe bound is at least 60%. Galactic Legends are used on offense only for squads nothing else can counter, and meta capital ships (Profundity, Executor, Leviathan) only when no other fleet counters.
Win rate, Battles, Safe bound
How often the counter wins in Kyber, over how many battles, and the cautious version after allowing for small samples. Strong means the safe bound is 75% or more, Acceptable means 60% to 75%.
Use this cron
Which of your datacrons to put on that squad, what perks it has and lacks, and the ideal perks with what they were measured to do. Each cron is used once.
Expired (red tag)
Their squad was built around a datacron set that no longer applies, so judge it by its units.
Set on defense
Squads from the units offense did not use: 15 character squads on a 3v3 board, 11 on 5v5, and 3 fleets. "Hold" is how often attackers lose to them. The report also shows where every Galactic Legend went, and none sits idle.
Meta defenses you can field
swgoh.gg's best-holding squads that you can build from what is left, with their Kyber hold. Offense avoids their units where it can.
If something goes wrong
Nothing happens, or "Open a swgoh.gg page first"
You are not on a swgoh.gg page. Click the swgoh.gg tab, then the bookmark.
swgoh.gg refused the request
You are not signed in, or your account cannot open GAC Insight. Sign in and open GAC Insight once to check.
Ally code not found, or no recent week with data
That opponent has no GAC history at this squad size, or the code is wrong. Use Choose another opponent.
It asks me questions
Either your bookmark is an older one (delete it and drag the button again), or your swgoh.gg account is not linked to a player (type your ally code once and it remembers).

On a phone

Not built for phones: bookmarks behave differently, and it will not run in the browsers inside apps like Discord, Reddit or Facebook. The easy way is to run it on a computer, press Download page, and send the file to your phone, where the report reads well. (Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone can work with a saved bookmark whose address is the console code.)

Good to know
  • It needs the opponent to have GAC history at this squad size. In a new season's first week the data is thin, which the safe bound shows, and balance changes made after the data was recorded are not reflected.
  • 3v3 and fleets are tested; 5v5 is not yet.
  • It uses swgoh.gg's own data, which can change without notice, and one run makes about 70 requests, so please do not run it over and over.
