GAC Counter Planner

Pick counters for an opponent's predicted defense, then build your own defense from the units you have left. It runs in your browser, with your own swgoh.gg login. Nothing is uploaded.

You need
  • A swgoh.gg account with GAC Insight access (Player+, or a guild with Guild Patron).
  • A desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).

Set it up once

  1. Sign in at swgoh.gg.
  2. Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar: GAC Planner

Use it

  1. Open any page on swgoh.gg and click the GAC Planner bookmark.
  2. Enter your ally code, your opponent's ally code, and 3 or 5 for 3v3 or 5v5.
  3. Wait about 30 seconds. The report opens on top of the page. Download page saves it.

Good to know

  • It reads the opponent's most recent week of the right size, so a brand-new opponent with no history cannot be planned.
  • Counters come from Kyber battles that used datacron sets still in play. Week one of a new season has thin data.
  • 5v5 is untested. Fleets and 3v3 are tested.
  • It uses swgoh.gg's own data endpoints, which can change without notice. If it stops working, that is the likely reason.
  • Be kind to the site: a run makes about 200 requests, paced out.
