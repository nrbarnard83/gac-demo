GAC Counter Planner

Predicts an opponent's defense, picks counters that really win, then builds your own defense from the units you have left. It runs in your browser with your own swgoh.gg login, and nothing is uploaded. See a sample report

Free to use, built by a guild leader in his spare time.If it saves you time in GAC, a small tip is welcome and never expected. Support this project
You need: a swgoh.gg account that can open GAC Insight (Player+, or a guild with Guild Patron) and a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).

Set up (once)

  1. Sign in at swgoh.gg in your browser.
  2. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).
  3. Drag this button up onto the bar and let go: GAC Planner
    Drag it, do not click it. Hold the mouse button on it, move up to the bookmarks bar, and release.

Run it

  1. Open any swgoh.gg page. It does nothing on a New Tab or any other site.
  2. Click the "GAC Planner" bookmark. It finds you and your opponent by itself: your current round's opponent, or your last one between weeks.
  3. Wait about 20 seconds. The report opens over the page. Download page saves it, and Choose another opponent switches who it plans against.
Reading the report
Their squad / Your counter
What the opponent is expected to place, and the squad of yours that beats it.
Win rate and Battles
How often that counter wins in Kyber, and how many battles that is based on.
Safe bound
The cautious version of the win rate, after allowing for how few battles it might rest on. A green badge means the safe bound is at least 75%.
Datacron
Which of your datacrons fits that squad. Each datacron is used once.
Expired (red tag)
The opponent's squad was built around a datacron set that no longer applies. Judge it by its units.
Your defense
Squads picked from the units not used on offense. "Hold" is how often attackers lose to that squad.
Meta defenses you can field
swgoh.gg's best-holding defense squads for the last season of this size, filtered to the ones you can build from what offense left. "Season hold" covers all leagues, "Kyber hold" is Kyber only when there is enough data. Offense avoids spending these squads' units where another solid counter exists.
If something goes wrong
I click the bookmark and nothing happens
You are probably not on a swgoh.gg page. Click the swgoh.gg tab first, then the bookmark.
It says "Open a swgoh.gg page first"
Same thing. Switch to a swgoh.gg tab and click again.
It says swgoh.gg refused the request
You are not signed in, or your account cannot open GAC Insight. Sign in, then open the GAC Insight page once to confirm.
It says it could not find that ally code
Check the opponent's ally code. Digits only, and it must be a player who has a swgoh.gg profile.
It says no recent week with data was found
That opponent has no recent GAC history of the squad size chosen. Use Choose another opponent.
It asks for my ally code
Your swgoh.gg account is not linked to a player. Type it in once and it will remember it.
My bookmark still asks questions
It is an older bookmark. Delete it and drag the button above onto the bar again.

On a phone

Phones treat bookmarks differently. It can work in Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone if you save a bookmark and replace its address with the code from the console method, then open it from the bookmarks menu while on a swgoh.gg page. It does not work in the browsers built into apps such as Discord, Reddit or Facebook.

The easy way: run it on a computer, press Download page, and send the file to your phone. The report reads well on a small screen.

Good to know
  • It reads the opponent's most recent week of the right squad size, so it cannot plan against a player with no GAC history.
  • Counters come from Kyber battles that used datacron sets still in play. In the first week of a new season there is little data, and the safe bound shows it.
  • Balance changes made after the battle data was recorded are not reflected.
  • 3v3 and fleets are tested. 5v5 is not yet.
  • It uses swgoh.gg's own data, which can change without notice. If it stops working, that is the likely reason.
  • One run makes about 70 requests, spaced out. Please do not run it over and over.
