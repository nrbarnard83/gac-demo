GAC Counter Planner

Give it your ally code and an opponent's. It predicts what that opponent will put on defense, picks counters that really win, and then builds your own defense from the units you have left. It runs inside your browser with your own swgoh.gg login. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored.

See a sample report first

Before you start, you need
  • A swgoh.gg account that can open GAC Insight (a Player+ subscriber, or a member of a guild with Guild Patron).
  • A computer with Chrome, Edge or Firefox. (Phones are covered at the bottom.)
  • Nothing else to look up: your account and your recent opponents are read from your swgoh.gg login. (To plan against someone outside your bracket, you can type their ally code, the 9 digits in swgoh.gg/p/123456789/.)

Set it up (once)

  1. Sign in at swgoh.gg in your browser, and check you can open the GAC Insight page.
  2. Show your bookmarks bar. It is the row of shortcuts under the address bar. If you cannot see it, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).
  3. Drag this button up onto the bookmarks bar and let go:
    GAC Planner
    Drag it, do not click it. Hold the mouse button down on it, move the mouse up to the bookmarks bar, and release. Clicking it here does nothing useful.

    You now have a bookmark called "GAC Planner". You never need to come back to this page unless you want the steps again.

Run it

It only works on a swgoh.gg page. It does nothing on a New Tab, on this page, or on any other website. Open swgoh.gg first.
  1. Open any swgoh.gg page in a tab, for example the home page, and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Click the "GAC Planner" bookmark. It already knows who you are from your swgoh.gg login, and it works out 3v3 or 5v5 from the season calendar, so it asks just one thing:
    • Who to plan against. It lists the opponents from your latest GAC week (with their guilds), then the other players in your bracket. Type the number, or type any ally code.
    The box also says which squad size it picked. If it is wrong, add a space and 3 or 5 after your answer, like "1 5". If you have more than one account linked to swgoh.gg, it first asks which one to use. Between GAC seasons the list shows last week's opponents, which is handy for a trial run.
  3. Wait about 30 seconds. A small black box in the corner shows what it is doing. Do not close the tab.
  4. Read the report. It opens over the page. Download page (top right) saves it as a file you can send to anyone. Close puts you back on swgoh.gg.

Reading the report

Their squad / Your counter
What the opponent is expected to place, and the squad of yours that beats it.
Win rate and Battles
How often that counter wins in Kyber, and how many battles that is based on.
Safe bound
The cautious version of the win rate, after allowing for how few battles it might rest on. A green badge means the safe bound is at least 75%. A counter based on only a handful of battles looks weaker here than its raw win rate.
Datacron
Which of your datacrons fits that squad. Each datacron is used once.
Expired (red tag)
The opponent's squad was built around a datacron set that no longer applies. Judge it by its units, not its old datacron.
Your defense
Squads picked from the units not used on offense. "Hold" is how often attackers lose to that squad.

If something goes wrong

I click the bookmark and nothing happens
You are probably not on a swgoh.gg page. Click the swgoh.gg tab first, then the bookmark.
It says "Open a swgoh.gg page first"
Same thing. Switch to a swgoh.gg tab and click again.
It says swgoh.gg refused the request
You are not signed in, or your account cannot open GAC Insight. Sign in, then open the GAC Insight page once to confirm.
It says it could not find that ally code
Check the opponent's ally code. Digits only, and it must be a player who has a swgoh.gg profile.
It asks for my ally code
Your swgoh.gg account is not linked to a player, so it could not read your code. Type it in once and it will remember it.
My bookmark still asks for my ally code
It is an older bookmark. Delete it and drag the button above onto the bookmarks bar again.
It says no recent week with data was found
That opponent has no recent GAC history of the squad size you chose. Try the other size, or another opponent.
Still stuck, or the bookmark will not drag
Use the console method below.

On a phone

Phones are the awkward part, because they treat bookmarks differently. It can work in Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone if you save a bookmark and replace its address with the code from the console method, then open it from the bookmarks menu while you are on a swgoh.gg page. It does not work in the browsers built into apps such as Discord, Reddit or Facebook.

The easy way: run it on a computer, press Download page, and send the file to your phone. The report is laid out to read well on a small screen.

Good to know

  • It reads the opponent's most recent week of the squad size you pick, so it cannot plan against a player with no GAC history.
  • Counters come from Kyber battles that used datacron sets still in play. In the first week of a new season there is little data, and the safe bound will show it.
  • 3v3 and fleets are tested. 5v5 is not yet.
  • It uses swgoh.gg's own data, which can change without notice. If it stops working, that is the likely reason.
  • One run makes about 200 requests, spaced out. Please do not run it over and over.
