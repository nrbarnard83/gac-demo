How it works

  1. Read their defense. The most recent week of their GAC history shows which squads they place, and with which datacrons.
  2. Find counters. GAC Insight win rates, Kyber only, live datacron sets only. Anything under 60% or fewer than 5 battles is thrown out.
  3. Be honest about sample size. Counters are ranked by the safe end of their win rate, so 5 wins out of 5 never beats thousands of battles.
  4. Spend little, keep strong. Each unit is used once, offense picks the cheapest solid counters, and what is left builds your defense.

Built-in safeguards, and what it cannot do yet

  • Expired datacron sets are always dropped, from the counters and from the opponent's squads. Week one of a new season has thin data, and the plan shows how thin.
  • One datacron per squad, never reused. Each is matched to the squads whose units its abilities target.
  • Not handled yet: 5v5, and predicting a brand-new opponent with no history.
  • Datacron fit is a simple score of how many of a squad's units each ability targets, not a full simulation.
